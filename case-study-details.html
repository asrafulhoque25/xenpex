The challenge

FinanceFlow's legacy platform was hemorrhaging users during critical touchpoints, resulting in significant revenue loss and customer dissatisfaction.

The problem

Manual Inventory Management Nightmare

  • 1

    60% User Drop-off During Onboarding

    The multi-step registration process was confusing and time-consuming, causing massive abandonment rates.

  • 2

    Complex Navigation Structure

    Users couldn't find key features. Average time to complete basic tasks was 8+ minutes.

  • 3

    Poor Mobile Experience

    85% of users accessed the platform via mobile, but the responsive design was broken and inconsistent.

  • 4

    High Support Ticket Volumee

    15,000+ monthly support tickets related to UI confusion and feature discoverability issues.

Project Objectivesn

Measurable Success Criteria

  • Reduce Onboarding Time to Under 2 Minutes

    Single source of truth with live inventory updates across all locations, accessible to authorized users from any device.

  • Increase Feature Discoverability by 75%

    Intelligent validation rules and automated data entry from barcode scans and API integrations with existing systems.

  • Achieve 4.5+ App Store Rating

    Machine learning-powered predictions for reorder points, preventing stockouts and optimizing warehouse space utilization.

  • Reduce Support Tickets by 40%

    Customizable reports generated instantly with export to Excel, PDF, or automated email delivery to stakeholders.
