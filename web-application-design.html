Where Product Logic Meets Intelligent UX

At Pixxen, we focus on Information Architecture (IA) first. Before any pixel is pushed, we map out complex user roles, data relationships, and business rules. This ensures your application's "brain" is structured for scalability, security, and seamless user experiences.

  • 1

    Information Architecture Mapping

    We create detailed IA diagrams that visualize how data flows through your system, ensuring logical hierarchies and intuitive navigation structures.

  • 2

    User Role & Permission Design

    Complex applications require sophisticated access control. We design permission systems that are secure yet flexible enough to adapt to your organizational structure.

  • 3

    Multi-Step Process Optimization

    From onboarding flows to complex approval workflows, we design each step to minimize friction while maintaining data integrity and compliance requirements.

Transforming Complex Data

See how we helped a logistics company replace spreadsheet chaos with an intelligent web application.

The problem

Manual Inventory Management Nightmare

  • Fragmented Data Across 12 Spreadsheets

    Team members maintaining separate Excel files with no real-time sync, leading to constant data conflicts and version control issues.

  • 6+ Hours Daily on Manual Data Entry

    Staff wasting valuable time copying information between systems, with a 15% error rate causing costly inventory discrepancies.

  • Zero Visibility Across 8 Warehouses

    No centralized view of inventory levels, making it impossible to optimize stock distribution or prevent stockouts.

  • Week-Long Reporting Process

    Generating monthly reports required manually consolidating data from multiple sources, taking 5-7 business days.

The solution

Custom Inventory Intelligence Platform

    Intelligent validation rules and automated data entry from barcode scans and API integrations with existing systems.
